## 1. How Ad Platforms Charge You (6 Core Pricing Models)

Think of online advertising like running an offline business. The ways platforms charge you fall into six basic rules, ordered from the moment someone sees your ad to the moment they pay you:

* **CPT (Cost Per Time):** Renting a billboard. Regardless of how many people see the ad or buy from you, you pay a flat fee by the day, week, or hour. Common for homepage banner takeovers, mobile app splash screens, and YouTube Masthead buyouts.
* **CPM (Cost Per Mille):** Flyer printing costs. You pay every time your ad appears on users' screens **1,000 times** (*mille* is Latin for thousand), whether they click or buy. Commonly used for new product launches and brand awareness.
* **CPV (Cost Per View):** Pay-per-watch. Used specifically for video ads—you only pay when a viewer watches for a set duration (such as 30 seconds, or to the end of a short video). If they scroll past in two seconds or hit "Skip," you pay nothing. Common on YouTube in-stream ads, TikTok/Douyin video promotion, and Bilibili.
* **CPC (Cost Per Click):** A store-entry fee per visitor. Showing the ad is free; you only pay when someone is interested enough to **click through** to your website or product page. (This entire category of click-based advertising is also broadly called **PPC**, or Pay Per Click.) Common on Google/Bing Search ads, news site recommendation widgets, and Amazon/Shopee keyword ads.
* **CPA (Cost Per Action):** Pay for results. Impressions and clicks are free—you only pay when a user completes a specific action you define, such as submitting a contact form, signing up, installing an app, or placing an order. When focused strictly on lead forms, it is often called **CPL (Cost Per Lead)**; for app downloads, **CPI (Cost Per Install)**.
* **CPS (Cost Per Sale):** Pure sales commission. Zero upfront ad spend—you only pay a fixed percentage (say, 15%) of the actual order value after a customer completes a purchase. No sale, no cost. Common in TikTok Shop creator affiliates, Amazon Associates, and cashback/coupon networks.

### Breaking Down the Three "CPM Brothers"

Modern social and short-video platforms (Meta, TikTok, Douyin) frequently display **CPM, oCPM, and eCPM** in their dashboards. Each serves a completely different role:

| Term | What It Is | Plain-English Meaning | Who Controls It? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **CPM** | **Bidding Method** (Pure Reach) | You tell the platform: "I'll pay $10 per 1,000 impressions for this audience." The platform delivers the views with zero guarantee of clicks or sales. | Advertiser sets manual bid |
| **oCPM** | **Smart Bidding** (Modern Standard) | **Billed by impression on the surface, priced by conversion goal underneath.** You tell the platform: "Find me buyers—I'm willing to pay $15 per order." The AI automatically bids higher for people likely to shop and skips non-buyers. You are still billed per actual impression, but average costs steer toward $15 per sale. | Advertiser sets target CPA; AI bids automatically |
| **eCPM** | **Evaluation Yardstick** (Platform Metric) | Since different advertisers bid by click, impression, or conversion, how does the platform decide who wins an ad slot? It converts every bid into **"how much revenue 1,000 impressions will generate for the platform."** Whoever has the highest eCPM wins the placement. | Calculated automatically by the system |

> When calculating eCPM, platforms multiply your bid by your expected click-through rate and conversion rate. If your image or video is twice as engaging as a competitor's (double the CTR), you can bid half as much, achieve the exact same eCPM, and win the traffic at half the cost.
> **Note:** **oCPC** (common in search and native ads) works the exact same way—you set a target cost per conversion, the system automatically adjusts each click bid, and you pay per actual click.

## 2. How to Read Ad Dashboards & Website Analytics

When you open any ad manager (such as popIn, Google Ads, or Meta), dozens of columns appear. You only need to track the core metrics below, arranged in funnel order from exposure to profit.

### Core Ad Dashboard Metrics (In Funnel Order)

* **IMP / Impressions:** Total number of times your ad appeared on a screen. Every time a page loads your ad slot, it counts as 1 impression (one person seeing it 3 times equals 3 impressions—not unique **Reach**).
* **Clicks:** Total number of times users clicked your ad to visit your website or landing page.
* **CTR (Click-Through Rate):** `Clicks ÷ IMP × 100%`. Measures how well your image, video hook, or headline attracts passersby ("curb appeal"). If 1,000 people see your ad and only 2 click (0.2% CTR), your creative isn't stopping the scroll.
* **CPC (Cost Per Click):** `Cost ÷ Clicks`. Average price paid per visitor click. Higher CTR directly drives down CPC.
* **Cost / Spend:** Total raw ad spend consumed during a period. Note that on many overseas or regional platforms (such as popIn), dashboard `Cost` figures are **pre-tax**; local VAT or service taxes are added at billing or invoicing.
* **CV / Conversions:** Total number of visitors who completed your desired goal after clicking (checkout, lead form submission, sign-up).
* **CVR (Conversion Rate):** `CV ÷ Clicks × 100%`. Measures how persuasive your landing page and offer are ("in-store closing ability"). If 100 people click through and 3 buy, your CVR is 3%.
* **CPA (Cost Per Acquisition / Action):** `Cost ÷ CV` (also equal to `CPC ÷ CVR`). Average ad spend required to win one customer or lead. If you raise your website's `CVR` from 1% to 3%, your acquisition cost drops by two-thirds without changing your ad bids.
* **Revenue:** Total gross sales generated by your ad campaigns.
* **AOV / ARPO (Average Order Value):** `Revenue ÷ Number of Orders`. Average dollar amount spent per order (modern e-commerce uses AOV; older telecom/gaming reports sometimes call it ARPO).
* **ROAS vs. ROI (Return Metrics):**
* **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend):** `Revenue ÷ Cost`. The standard top-line metric in ad dashboards. Spending $100 in ads to generate $350 in sales equals a ROAS of **3.5** (or 350%). **ROAS measures gross revenue only—it ignores product cost and shipping.**
* **ROI (Return on Investment):** Net profit return after subtracting product costs (COGS), shipping, taxes, and ad spend. If your product has a 50% gross margin, a ROAS of 2.0 merely breaks even; you need 3.0+ to make real profit.


* **Frequency:** `IMP ÷ Reach`. Average number of times each unique person has seen your ad. In social feeds, if frequency climbs past **2.5–3.0** and CPA rises, your audience is fatigued and needs fresh creatives immediately.
* **KPI (Key Performance Indicator):** The target benchmark used to judge campaign success (e.g., keeping CPA under $15 or ROAS above 3.0).

### Website Traffic Basics (Evaluating Landing Page Engagement)

Once ads bring visitors to your site, three standard analytics terms measure how they behave:

* **PV (Page View):** Total number of pages loaded. If one visitor refreshes a page 5 times or views 5 different pages, PV equals **5**.
* **UV (Unique Visitor):** Number of distinct individuals visiting your site within a day (tracked via cookies/device ID). One person visiting 10 times in a single day counts as **1 UV**.
* **Visit / Session:** Number of complete browsing sessions on your site. If someone visits in the morning, views 3 pages, and closes the tab (1 Visit, 3 PVs), then returns that evening (another Visit), total Visits equal **2**. High Visits with an average of 1 PV per visit means people are bouncing immediately.

### Three-Step Troubleshooting Rule of Thumb

* **No Impressions (IMP near zero):** Your bid is too low to win auctions, or your location/schedule/audience targeting is too narrow.
* **Impressions, Few Clicks (Low CTR):** Your ad image, opening 3 seconds of video, or headline fails to grab attention—swap creatives instead of forcing higher bids.
* **Clicks, Few Sales (Low CVR):** The ad worked, but the landing page failed—check for slow load times (over 3 seconds), message mismatch between ad and page, weak social proof, or a clunky checkout form.

## 3. Clarifying Four Commonly Confused Terms: SEM, In-Feed, Ad Networks & DSPs

Beginners often treat these four terms as competing ad categories, when they actually describe **four completely different layers of advertising**—like going out to eat: **"which restaurant you visit (SEM)," "how the dish is plated (In-Feed)," "pre-packaged combos from a central kitchen (Ad Network)," and "an automated smart-ordering system (DSP)."**

| Dimension | SEM (Search Engine Marketing) | In-Feed Ads | Ad Network | DSP (Demand-Side Platform) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Core Identity** | **Search marketing** within search engines | **Native-looking ads** blended into content feeds | **Traffic wholesaler** bundling small sites/apps | **Automated buying software** picking users cross-web |
| **Classification Angle** | By **traffic entry point** | By **ad format / appearance** | By **inventory aggregation** | By **programmatic buying tech** |
| **Where Ads Run** | Search engines only | Social, short-video, and news feeds | Hundreds of thousands of third-party sites/apps | Across all connected sites, apps, and networks |
| **How It Finds Buyers** | **Keywords** (active search intent) | **Platform algorithms** (in-app behavior) | **Site categories** (buying site bundles) | **Cross-site tracking** (following individual users) |
| **Representative Platforms** | Google Ads, Bing, Baidu | Meta, TikTok, Douyin, Xiaohongshu | Google GDN, Pangolin, Youlianghui | The Trade Desk, Criteo, Google DV360 |

* **SEM (Search Engine Marketing):** All marketing done on search engines (Google, Bing, Baidu). It splits into **paid search ads (PPC)** and free **SEO (Search Engine Optimization)**, built around active "people looking for products" intent.
* **In-Feed Ads:** Ads formatted to look almost identical to surrounding organic content inside social feeds, short-video apps, or news lists (covering both social platforms like Meta/TikTok and news recommendation widgets like popIn/Taboola). Built around passive "products finding people" discovery.
* **Ad Network:** An intermediary marketplace that signs up hundreds of thousands of independent websites, blogs, and mobile apps, packages their ad slots by vertical (e.g., finance sites, casual games), and sells them in bulk to advertisers (such as Google Display Network, Pangolin, or Youlianghui; native platforms like popIn, Dable, and Taboola are essentially specialized news ad networks).
* **DSP (Demand-Side Platform):** Next-generation automated media-buying software (such as The Trade Desk or Criteo). Instead of buying pre-packaged website bundles, a DSP connects to open ad exchanges and targets **specific individuals** (like someone who abandoned a cart on your store yesterday). Whenever that user opens any participating weather app or news site, the DSP bids and serves your ad in under **0.1 seconds** via Real-Time Bidding (RTB).

> **How All Four Work Together in Real Life:**
> 1. You want a home espresso machine, so you search "best home espresso machine" on Google and click the top sponsored link—that is **SEM (Search Ads)**.
> 2. You leave without buying. That night, while scrolling Instagram or TikTok, a video review of that same machine appears between regular posts—that is an **In-Feed Ad**.
> 3. You still hold off. The next day, you open a free mobile game or local news site and see a banner ad at the bottom—that ad slot belongs to an **Ad Network**.
> 4. Remarkably, that banner shows the exact machine you viewed yesterday with a 10% off coupon—the system that recognized you, won the auction in 0.1 seconds, and served that personalized reminder is a **DSP**.

## 4. Six Major Ad Types, Pricing Models & Playbooks

Based on user browsing behavior, paid advertising divides into six primary channels:

### 1. Search Ads (Primary Pricing: CPC / oCPC)

* **How You Pay:** Cost Per Click (CPC). Showing up on the search results page is free; you only pay when a searcher clicks through to your site (modern campaigns also use oCPC/tCPA smart bidding).
* **Channel Dynamics:** Active intent ("people looking for solutions"). Users type specific problems or product names into a search bar at the peak of buying intent.
* **Typical Platforms:** Google Search, Microsoft Ads (Bing), Yahoo Search, Baidu, 360 Search, Sogou.
* **Operational Playbook:**
* **Segment Keywords by Intent:** Separate campaigns into **Brand Keywords** (low-cost defense against competitors poaching your name), **Core Category Terms** (main traffic drivers), and **Long-Tail Problem/Comparison Terms** (e.g., "how to choose X" or "A vs. B," which convert highest).
* **Broad Match + Smart Bidding:** Pair Broad Match keywords with Target CPA (tCPA) or Target ROAS (tROAS) so Google's AI captures nuanced long-tail queries automatically.
* **Daily Negative Keyword Scrubbing:** Review Search Terms reports daily and block irrelevant queries ("free," "tutorial," "repair," "used," "jobs") to stop wasted spend.
* **Maximize Ad Assets:** Fill out sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets, pricing, and image extensions to dominate screen real estate and boost CTR.



### 2. Native Content Recommendation Ads (Primary Pricing: CPC / oCPC)

* **How You Pay:** Primarily CPC—you pay nothing until a reader clicks your article card (select platforms also offer oCPC).
* **Channel Dynamics:** Editorial soft-selling ("reading the news and discovering a solution"). Ads sit at the bottom or mid-article of news portals disguised as "Recommended Reading," reaching older, higher-intent readers with patience for text.
* **Typical Platforms:** popIn, Dable, LINE TODAY native placements (Asia-Pacific/Chinese markets); Taboola, Outbrain, MGID, Revcontent (Global/Western markets).
* **Operational Playbook:**
* **Use an Advertorial Pre-Lander:** News readers reject hard-sell product pages. Never link straight to a checkout page—send clicks to a story-driven editorial page (news report, expert breakdown, or customer journey) that builds trust before linking to the product page.
* **De-Advertised Visuals:** Avoid polished studio banners. Use authentic phone photos, close-up product shots, or silent looping GIFs (Motion Ads) paired with curiosity-driven news headlines.
* **Publisher / Site Optimization:** Traffic spreads across hundreds of partner sites. Export site-level reports regularly, **blacklist** high-click/zero-sale sites, and apply positive **bid modifiers** to high-converting news portals.



### 3. Social & Short-Video In-Feed Ads (Primary Pricing: oCPM / CPM / CPV)

* **How You Pay:** Primarily **oCPM** (billed per 1,000 impressions while AI bids toward your target CPA/ROAS). Pure awareness/takeover campaigns use flat **CPM** or **CPT**; pure video-view campaigns use **CPV**.
* **Channel Dynamics:** Passive discovery ("products finding people"). Users are scrolling for entertainment with zero shopping plans; thumb-stopping visuals interrupt attention and spark impulse purchases.
* **Typical Platforms:** Meta (Facebook / Instagram), TikTok Ads, YouTube Shorts, Snapchat, Pinterest, X (Twitter); Douyin/Ocean Engine, Tencent Ads (WeChat Moments/Channels), Xiaohongshu, Kuaishou.
* **Operational Playbook:**
* **Simplify Account Structure:** Avoid over-segmenting ages and interest tags. Use **Broad Targeting** or automated shopping campaigns (Meta ASC, TikTok Smart+) so the algorithm has enough conversion volume to learn.
* **Win the First 3 Seconds (The Hook):** Social feeds burn through creatives fast. Open every video with a sharp pain point, counterintuitive claim, or dramatic before-and-after visual to stop the scroll.
* **Set Up Server-Side Tracking (CAPI / Events API):** Browser pixels lose 20–40% of conversion data due to privacy limits. Send verified purchase events directly from your server to train the ad algorithm accurately.



### 4. Shopping & E-Commerce Marketplace Ads (Primary Pricing: CPC / oCPM)

* **How You Pay:** A mix of **CPC** and **oCPM**. Search-driven shelf ads (Amazon SP, Shopee Keyword Ads, Taobao Zhitongche, standard Google Shopping) charge per click (CPC); dynamic catalog feeds (Meta DPA, Google Performance Max) use oCPM to hit target ROAS.
* **Channel Dynamics:** Shelf comparison ("ready to compare and buy"). Ads display product photos, prices, star ratings, and promo badges directly to shoppers comparing options.
* **Typical Platforms:** Google Shopping (and PMax), Meta Dynamic Product Ads (DPA), Criteo; marketplace tools like Amazon Sponsored Products, Shopee Ads, Taobao Zhitongche, Pinduoduo Search.
* **Operational Playbook:**
* **Optimize Your Product Feed:** Algorithms match shoppers using your feed data, not manual keywords. Put core search attributes at the front of product titles and use crisp white-background or lifestyle main images.
* **Segment Bids by Profit Margin:** Group SKUs into high-margin winners, traffic drivers, and clearance items, assigning looser ROAS targets to high-margin items so they scale aggressively.
* **Dynamic Retargeting:** Automatically serve exact product cards to visitors who viewed or added items to their cart without checking out.



### 5. Display / Banner Ads (Primary Pricing: CPM / CPC)

* **How You Pay:** CPM or CPC for banner images placed across website headers, sidebars, and mobile app screens.
* **Channel Dynamics:** Broad reach and cross-site retargeting ("following visitors across the web"). Cold traffic conversion is very low; its real strength is cheaply reminding past visitors to return and finish buying.
* **Typical Platforms:** Google Display Network (GDN), The Trade Desk (TTD), Criteo, Baidu Baiyi, Tencent Youlianghui, Pangolin.
* **Operational Playbook:**
* **Focus on Retargeting:** Aim display budgets at warm visitors who browsed your site or abandoned carts rather than cold audiences.
* **Exclude Mobile App Inventory:** Bulk-exclude gaming and kids' mobile apps in placement settings to prevent accidental thumb-clicks from draining your budget.
* **Cap Impression Frequency:** Limit delivery to 2–3 impressions per user per day to avoid banner blindness and user annoyance.



### 6. Affiliate Marketing & Creator Commission Ads (Primary Pricing: CPS / CPA)

* **How You Pay:** Pure performance commission (**CPS** percentage of completed sales, or **CPA/CPI** flat fee per verified lead/install). Zero upfront media spend.
* **Channel Dynamics:** Zero-risk distribution ("pay only after it sells"). You list your product and commission terms on a network so review bloggers, coupon sites, and creators promote it for you.
* **Typical Platforms:** Impact, CJ Affiliate, ShareASale, Rakuten Advertising, ClickBank; creator programs like TikTok Shop Affiliate, Amazon Associates, YouTube Shopping Affiliate, Douyin Affiliate.
* **Operational Playbook:**
* **Build Tiered Commission Incentives:** Act like a channel partnership manager—offer a base commission (e.g., 15%), volume bonuses (20% above $5,000/month), and exclusive creator discount codes to earn top placement.
* **Supply Ready-to-Use Creative Kits:** Upload proven high-converting images, comparison charts, and video scripts to the affiliate portal to lower creator friction.
* **Audit Attribution Against Fraud:** Monitor click-to-conversion timestamps and reject commissions from rogue browser extensions that inject hidden cookies at checkout to hijack organic sales.

## 5. Quick-Pick Matrix: Choosing Channels for a New Project

Use this table whenever launching a new product or business line to pick your starting channel:

| Your Product / Business Scenario | Best Pricing Model | Priority Platforms | #1 Key to Success |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Clear problem/solution; customers actively search online** | **CPC** (Search) | Google Search, Bing, Baidu | Keyword intent precision + daily negative keywords |
| **Novel, visual, impulse-buy consumer product** | **oCPM** (In-Feed) | Meta (FB/IG), TikTok, Douyin, Xiaohongshu | Strong 3-second video hooks + fast creative testing |
| **Higher ticket, health/beauty/functional item needing explanation** | **CPC** (Native) | Dable, popIn, Taboola, Outbrain | Authentic advertorial pre-lander + blacklisting weak sites |
| **Standard retail SKU competing on price and product photo** | **CPC / oCPM** (Shopping) | Google Shopping (PMax), Amazon, Shopee | Optimized Product Feed titles/images + margin-based bidding |
| **High website traffic with heavy cart abandonment** | **CPM / oCPM** (Retargeting) | Google GDN, Criteo, Meta Dynamic Ads | Cross-site product reminders + excluding mobile game apps |
| **Proven bestseller ready to scale with zero ad-spend risk** | **CPS / CPA** (Affiliate) | TikTok Shop Affiliate, Impact, ShareASale | Attractive tiered commissions + boosting top creator videos |
