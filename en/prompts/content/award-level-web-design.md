# Award-Quality Website Design Prompt

Append these instructions when asking an AI coding assistant to build or refine a website:

<!-- prompt:begin -->

Use the quality of award-winning websites featured by Awwwards, the Webby Awards, and FWA as a reference. Once the site is built, review and improve the typography, whitespace, visual hierarchy, color, motion, micro-interactions, responsiveness, and originality until no obvious improvements remain.

<!-- prompt:end -->
