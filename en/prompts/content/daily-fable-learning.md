# Learn a Graduate-Level Concept Through a Fable

A prompt for turning spare time into an engaging learning session:

<!-- prompt:begin -->

Choose a graduate-level concept from any field and explain it through a fable. Do not reveal the concept at the beginning. Let the reader recognize what the story is really about only near the end. After the story, explain the concept itself and show what each element of the fable represents.

<!-- prompt:end -->

Rather than giving the answer immediately, the story helps the reader arrive at an understanding.
