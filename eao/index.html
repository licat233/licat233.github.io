01 / Where company work gets stuck

Companies rarely lack knowledge. Work gets stuck finding, asking andorganizing it.

Most companies already have files, policies, product information and years of experience. The problem is that this context is scattered across systems, folders and people — and generic AI cannot use what it cannot reach.

Information is scattered

Specifications, quotations, policies and historical files live across shared drives, documents, wikis and personal folders.

AI does not know the company

Generic AI may be capable, but it does not automatically know your products, rules, history or ways of working.

Too much repetitive preparation

Employees repeatedly search, collect, reformat and prepare the same kinds of information.

Knowledge depends on people

Important experience often lives with a few long-tenured employees, so others have to keep asking.

02 / What changes with EAO

Not another chatbot. Less friction between people and company information.

03 / A real work pattern

When a customer asks a product question, Sales should not start by hunting for files.

Traditional workflow

common today
  1. Guess where the information might be
  2. Search specifications and shared folders
  3. Ask the colleague who knows the product
  4. Find prior quotations or similar orders
  5. Manually assemble the customer reply

With EAO

EAO
  1. Ask the Sales Assistant
  2. EAO checks company product knowledge
  3. Read approved specification files and history
  4. Prepare a reply draft with company context
  5. Sales reviews and sends
EAO is not designed to remove people from the process. It is designed to take on the searching, context gathering and repetitive preparation that slows them down.
04 / Department use

Different departments have different work. They should not all receive the same generic AI.

Sales

Spend less time finding product information, prior quotations and reply context, leaving more time for customers.

Procurement

Assist with BOMorganizing, supplier information, historical purchasing context and cost preparation instead of starting from zero.

Marketing

Reuse brand knowledge, prior content and research material instead of repeating the same discovery work.

Finance

Find authorized policies, files and finance knowledge faster while preserving department access boundaries.

Operations

Retrieve internal procedures, documents and prior practices, reducing recurring informationorganizing.

Management

Access authorized cross-department information and prepare material for reviews, decisions and management communication.

05 / Why a company can stay in control

AI can enter the company without taking control away from the company.

Self-hosted system

The EAO application stack can run on infrastructure controlled by the organization.

Department boundaries

Different departments can have different knowledge, file and tool scopes.

Tools are explicitly approved

What an Agent can do depends on the tools and permissions the organization makes available.

Human review where it matters

Work that requires judgment or formal submission can keep employees in the confirmation loop.

External data handling still depends on the model providers and integrations selected by the organization. Self-hosting the EAO application does not automatically mean every model request stays local.
Real-company reference
