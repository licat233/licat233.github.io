02 / AI for every department

Start where the work already happens.

EAO is organized around departments and real tasks rather than one generic company chatbot.

Sales

Find product information, prepare customer replies, retrieve prior quotations and draft sales material.

Procurement

Break down BOMs, find supplier and product information, prepare cost analysis and draft quotations.

Marketing

Use brand knowledge, research topics, prepare content and reuse approved company context.

Finance

Work with authorized finance knowledge, policies and files within configured access boundaries.

Operations

Find internal documents, procedures and operational knowledge; assist with recurring office work.

Management

Access company knowledge across authorized scopes and prepare material for decisions and reviews.

03 / Keep your systems

Keep your systems. Add AI around them.

EAO does not ask a company to replace mature business systems. It connects AI to the systems, files and knowledge the organization already relies on.

The organizational layer between AI and the company.

EAO focuses on the layer most AI tools leave to the enterprise: departments, authority, company context, files, execution boundaries and operational ownership.

04 / Your company stays in control

Bring AI in without creating a parallel organization.

Self-hosted system

The EAO application stack can run under the organization's own infrastructure.

Existing access boundaries

EAO is designed to preserve existing access boundaries rather than bypass them.

Department Profiles

Departments can have separate knowledge, tools and execution scopes.

Approved tools

Agent capabilities depend on the tools and permissions explicitly made available.

Recoverable operations

Operational changes are designed to be inspectable, bounded and recoverable.

External data handling depends on the model providers and integrations selected by the organization. Self-hosting EAO does not by itself mean every model request remains local.
05 / Start small

Use the lightest sufficient execution path.

EAO does not require a company to build a large AI platform on day one. Start with the smallest useful deployment and add capability only when the work needs it.
